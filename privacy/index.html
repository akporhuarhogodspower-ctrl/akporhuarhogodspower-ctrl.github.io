PiBooks - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

PiBooks by SHADAVELI COMPANY is a book marketplace like Amazon KDP, built on Pi. African authors publish books and readers buy with Pi.

What we collect

We collect only what Pi provides for sign-in: your Pi username and Pi wallet address. We do not collect your Pi password. Payments are processed by Pi Network.

What we use it for

To show your library, your purchases, and to pay authors 70% royalty. We do not sell your data.
